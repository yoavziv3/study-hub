# Ingest pipeline (`server/lib/ingest.js`)

Files arrive via upload (`/api/upload`), the watched `inbox/` folder (`inbox/<courseId>/` forces the course; processed files move to `inbox/נקלטו/`), or Moodle sync. A single in-process queue runs stages `converting → rendering → extracting → classifying → analyzing → ready` (`STAGES`; labels mirrored in client `STAGE_LABELS`). Conversion uses Microsoft PowerPoint (AppleScript) or LibreOffice `soffice` for PPTX, poppler `pdftoppm`/`pdftotext` for rendering/text (`server/lib/extract.js`). Kind and lesson number are inferred from file names with the regexes at the top of `ingest.js`. Unfinished items resume on startup (`resumePending`).

**What goes on the site (the owner's rule):** only what is on Moodle. Lectures get AI study material; **tutorials don't** (the owner: a summary of a worked-examples sheet isn't worth the time – they go up as slides and files, and `aiKindOk` in `server/lib/courses.js` is the one rule: no AI for tutorials or exams, and a course's `aiKinds` can narrow it further; tutorials that already have content keep it); past exams (`kind: 'exam'`) and files marked `noAi` go up as they are – `noAi` is set automatically for scanned files (less than `MIN_TEXT` characters of text, `noAiReason: 'scanned'`) and by hand for question banks that span the whole course (`'bank'`), and `retry-ai` skips them. Generated practice (`POST /api/courses/:id/practice`) takes its style from the course's homework and tutorials (or its lectures before there are any) but may only cover topics already taught: the prompt gets a `taught_so_far` list from the lectures and tutorials on Moodle – never get ahead of the course. `meta.held` keeps an item on the Mac but out of every list (`listLectures`). **Question banks** (`server/lib/practice.js`): each lecture/tutorial/prep unit is grown to `BANK_TARGET` (200) questions in rounds of 20, each round focused on another stretch of its slides, kept in `content.extraQuiz` (checked by `checkQuiz`, near-duplicates dropped). They run on their own worker (`queueBank`) – after ingest, and for existing lessons via `POST /api/maintenance/grow-banks` – so a new lesson is published before its bank fills in; a usage limit or sign-out stops the run until the next one. Each round is written by **Claude (Sonnet, `AI_BANK_MODEL`) at night** (`BANK_CLAUDE_HOURS`, Israel time, default `1-7`, at most `BANK_CLAUDE_CAP` = 40 rounds a night, counted in `logs/bank-state.json`; a usage limit ends Claude's night) and **by Gemini at any other hour** within the share of its free daily quota not kept for lessons (`geminiRoom`; also at night once Claude's cap is used or it fails): `bankWriters()` in `practice.js` says who may write now, and the AI watch resumes the banks whenever either answers. Every CLI call is killed after 20 minutes (`CLI_TIMEOUT` in `ai.js`) so a hung call can't block a queue. Students practise in sets of 15 (`PracticeTab` in `Lecture.jsx`: unanswered, then wrong, then the rest) with "עוד 15 שאלות" at the end. A set in progress survives a refresh: `Quiz`'s `persistKey` saves the shuffled set and the place in it in localStorage (lesson practice and the course practice test). `quizCount` on lecture cards counts both `quiz` and `extraQuiz`. A question whose wording points at something on a slide ("לפי הטבלה בשקף 7", "בתרשים שהוצג") gets `showSlide` from `slideRef` in `checks.js` (run by `checkQuiz` on every new question; a slide number named in the text wins over the model's `slide`), and `Quiz` shows that slide inside the question (`SlidePeek inline`); `POST /api/maintenance/slide-refs` re-marks existing questions (and escapes their currency dollars). `fixSlide`/`matchSlide` (`checks.js`) also correct the model's slide number by text: the words of the question and its right answer (formulas left out) are compared with each slide's text, and the claim moves only when another slide matches clearly (≥ 0.4) and the claimed ±1 clearly doesn't (< 0.25, gain ≥ 0.2); questions with fewer than 6 distinct words keep their slide, a link is never dropped (image-only slides – diagrams, dashboards – have no text to match), and a slide named in the question text always wins. A question that leans on data it doesn't carry ("בנתוני שני השדות", "בדוגמת שני העובדים" – `DATA_REF` in `checks.js`) gets `showSlide` too, so the slide with that data appears inside it; `needsContext` marks questions that a reading of the whole bank (Claude, in batches) found to lean on a table, case or exercise they don't carry ("מר שמיר", "בתרגיל 2"): `markSlideRefs` keeps `showSlide` on for them whatever the wording. Open questions and flashcards have no slide field, so their context is a line in the text ("הקשר: …, שקפים …"). a question that leans on another question (`ANOTHER_Q`, "בהמשך לשאלה הקודמת") is dropped, since sets are shuffled (`checkQuiz` for new ones, `markSlideRefs` for existing ones). The question prompts carry `SELF_CONTAINED` (every question must make sense alone). `checkQuiz` also warns when under 20% of a set is `hard` (the prompts ask for a third hard, a third medium, a third easy). **Currency vs math:** `shared/currency.js` `escapeCurrency` turns every `$` that isn't part of a real formula (one line, no Hebrew inside, not opening/closing on a space, not followed by a digit) into `\$` – the client runs it before rendering (`Markdown`, `RichText`, `plainText`) and `checkQuiz` on new questions, so prices like "67$ … 1.6$" never pair up into a garbled formula.

**Exam-prep units** (`kind: 'prep'`) are lessons from an earlier semester that this one hasn't reached, imported as text only (no deck, `originalFile: null`): `node scripts/import-prep.js <courseId> <dir> "<semester>"` posts `slides-NN.txt` (+ optional `transcript-NN.txt`) per lesson to `POST /api/courses/:id/prep` → `addPrepItem`, which saves the text as `slides.json` and the transcript as `transcript.txt` (gitignored, stays on the Mac) and queues it; `prepPrompt` writes the usual study content from both. They are not counted as lessons (listed under the course's "הכנה למבחן" tab, `PrepUnits`), have no slides tab, and their quizzes and cards join the course's exam practice. The IS course's units (from its NotebookLM, semester B תשפ"ו) are held: the owner wants only what is on Moodle.

**Topics cut from a course book:** a lecture's `meta.noNotes` (no structured notes), `noBank` (no question bank) and `announced: true` (no "new material" push per topic) keep a book that is split into many topics from costing a bank and a notes call each; the AI writes only the summary, topics, glossary, cards and first questions. Linear algebra's lectures are the lecturer's notes (Dr. Nadav Meir, chapters 0–4, cut into eight topics) and calculus's are the course book from its Moodle link (teachingcenter.bgu.ac.il/books/calculus, `Book_Draft.pdf`, cut into fifteen) – until real lecture summaries are posted on Moodle; students add their own lectures with "הקבצים שלי".

**Which courses the site carries:** the owner's five (calculus 1, accounting, economics, physics, information systems) and linear algebra for engineering – nothing else. A Moodle course of another student is built from the cloud's list only if its name matches one of these (`subjectOk` in `server/lib/courses.js`, applied in `syncRemote`; another group of the same course, like physics groups 2 and 3, counts). English, Java, probability, Hebrew, scientific writing, "calculus ג1", "linear algebra ג" and the management versions of accounting and economics were removed and sit in `remoteIgnore`; their lesson folders were moved to `quarantine/` (git-ignored), not deleted. AI summaries only for the six (other groups of physics have `ai: false`) – and not for all of them: **calculus and linear algebra lectures get no AI any more** (`aiKinds: []`; the big book-topic summaries already cover them, new Moodle lectures go up as they are), and **physics** (`aiFrom: 'upload'`, `aiBy: 'gemini'`) summarises only decks the owner gives directly (the web upload or `inbox/phys/`), never what the Moodle sync brings, and writes them with Gemini alone (`generateLesson({ only })`). `aiKindOk(course, kind, meta)` is the one rule for all of this. **One summary per lecture across groups** (`server/lib/reuse.js`): when another group's deck of the same subject reads the same (≥ 80 % word overlap – never by lecture number, since two lecturers don't teach the same thing in lesson 1), it gets the existing content instead of a model call (`generator: 'reused'`); slide pointers are dropped unless the decks are the same size and ≥ 95 % alike.

**Linear algebra** (`m67024`, the regular course, built from other students' enrollments, `remote: true`; the finished prep programme's "ההתחלה" pages – linear algebra 66384, calculus 66385, Java 66386 – and English 68557 were removed and sit in `remoteIgnore` of the private `data/moodle.json`, so the sync never rebuilds them) has `ai: true, aiKinds: ['lecture'], bank: false`: AI summary, questions and cards for lectures only – no tutorials, no homework, no question banks (`bankOk`) – so the free Gemini quota isn't spent on a course the owner isn't in. Other remote courses stay `ai: false`. Check each generated summary against the pages (the owner can't read them): the model adds illustrative examples, which are marked "דוגמה להמחשה" / "להשלמה" when they aren't in the lecturer's document. Explanations never refer to an option by its place (`unorder` in `checks.js`, run by `checkQuiz` and the prompts' `SELF_CONTAINED`): options are shuffled for the student.

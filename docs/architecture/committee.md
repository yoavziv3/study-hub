# The degree committee's view (owner and committee members)

`/committee` ("מערכת הועד", nav item and route only when `!STATIC || me.committee` – the owner, or a member the owner added) lays every course's weekly meetings over each other to find hours when nobody has class (make-up lectures, tutorials, office hours). `server/lib/committee.js` keeps `data/committee.json` (published like other data): `extra` = courses the site doesn't carry (linear algebra, read from the catalog by `fetchCatalog`, refreshed every 12 hours or `POST /api/committee/refresh`) and `lines` = the registration paths the faculty offered the cohort per course (`[lecture]` or `[lecture, tutorial]` group numbers; physics is empty until its list is known). The cloud exports it in `build-static.js` and `gate.js` serves `/data/committee.json` to committee users only (404 for anyone else); locally `/api/committee`. **Members** (`cloud/lib/committee.js`, D1 table `committee_members` by Moodle user id, `isCommittee` = owner or member) get that page and nothing else of what the owner sees: `me.committee` in `/api/me`, `/data/committee.json`, and `/data/committee-courses.json` (every non-remote course's name, colour, term and weekly meetings – no material; the page uses it instead of the student's own courses in the cloud, so a member needn't be enrolled). Not given to members: system status, other students' courses and material (`visibleCourseIds`), other students' portal notices, the cohort filtering of the updates feed and the exams page's committee overview – those stay `owner`. Add or remove members from the Mac with `node --env-file=.env scripts/committee-members.js [add|remove <moodle-username> …]` (admin channel `/api/admin/committee`; the person must have signed in once; the table is part of the weekly D1 backup and `restore-backup.js`). `client/src/lib/committee.js` derives all possible lines from a course's catalog groups (tutorial 21 belongs to lecture 2) and `occupancy` assumes the cohort splits evenly over a course's selected lines: a cell's busy share is 1 − Π(1 − share per course). Which lines count is stored in the owner's own progress (`progress.committee.lines`).

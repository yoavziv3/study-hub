# BGU student portal (each student's real groups and room changes)

The portal (portal.bgu.ac.il) is the only per-student source of groups and timetable — Moodle has neither (no groups in academic courses, merged "01" course codes, no timetable events). Its firewall (AWS CloudFront geo-restriction) answers only from Israel — even a Cloudflare Worker placed near Tel Aviv is blocked — so a reader in Israel does it: an always-on machine running `relay/run.mjs` — an **old Android phone at home** (Termux service, installed over SSH with `./scripts/deploy-portal-android.sh <phone-ip>`) or a VM in Israel (`./scripts/deploy-portal-vm.sh ubuntu@<ip>`); both scripts first check that the portal answers from there — with the **Mac** as backup (`server/lib/portal-relay.js` asks with `&backup=1` and gets nothing while any other reader was seen in the last 3 minutes). Cloud options tried and dropped: a Cloudflare Worker (blocked even when placed near Tel Aviv), AWS Lambda in il-central-1 (the AWS "Projects" account can't opt in to regions), Oracle Cloud (sign-in trouble). Both run `shared/portal-relay-core.js`: poll `/api/admin/portal/jobs`, sign in only when the stored portal session (encrypted by the reader with a key derived from the private key, kept opaque in `portal_state`) has ended (`shared/portal-api.js`: `StandardLogin` → bearer token + the cookies it sets → `umbraco/Surface/MainPortalSurface/GetPageData` with `pageUrl: 'academy/schedule-and-courses'` → the `StudentCoursesAndScheduleBlockList` block's `scheduleByDays`; `Portal/Api/Home/Index` is only the home page), and posts the lessons to `/api/admin/portal/result`. The student's sign-in (username, password, ID) is sealed in their browser with the Mac's RSA-OAEP public key (`PORTAL_PUBLIC_KEY` in `wrangler.toml`; private key in `portal-key.json`, created by `node scripts/portal-key.js`, not in git, and copied to the reader machine) — the cloud stores it in `portal_links` but can't open it. A failed sign-in sets `ok = 0` and is never retried. The cloud derives the student's groups from the lessons (`shared/groups.js` `groupsFromPortal`: day/time, then room numbers, then BGU numbering — tutorial 21 belongs to lecture 2); those groups override hand-picked ones in notifications, and the timetable page copies them into `progress.classGroups`. The student's own portal notices also join their updates feed in the cloud: `gate.js` adds `portalFeed` (`portal.js`) to `/data/updates.json` – items of type `portal`, keyed `portal:<noticeKey>`, last 30 days, with Claude's reading and the course found by `courseOfLesson` (`shared/groups.js`: name or code, else the one course with a catalog meeting of that kind at that day and time). They are built per request from that student's `portal_links` row and never published, so no one sees another student's notices. `forOtherGroups` (same file) drops course announcements aimed at other groups, both from phone notifications and (hidden by default) from the updates feed. A portal lesson is matched to a site course by a telling word of its name (`sameCourse` in `shared/groups.js` ignores words many names share – מבוא, יסודות, להנדסה… – after "מבוא לפיזיקה" was taken for "מבוא לכלכלה"), its code, or its day and time. Courses the catalog lists without meeting times (physics: only group numbers) can't get a group from the portal; their lessons still show on the timetable as the portal has them (`portalOnlySlots` in `client/src/lib/schedule.js`, no group). A student who hasn't connected the portal picks groups by hand – the connect card sits at the top of the timetable. A wrong portal password must not return HTTP 401 — the client treats 401 as "signed out of this site". Local tests: `mock-portal` launch config, `PORTAL_URL` in `.dev.vars`, and a relay run with `CLOUD_URL=http://localhost:8788`.
